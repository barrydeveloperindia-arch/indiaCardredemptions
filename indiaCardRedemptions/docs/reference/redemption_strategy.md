## 🗺️ Trip 1: London (The "Economy Duo" Play)
*Since you need 2 tickets, Business Class is mathematically out of reach without paying huge out-of-pocket cash. We optimize for Economy to save money.*

* **The Outbound (Qatar):** Use your **90,000 Qatar Avios**. A one-way Economy ticket to London is ~35,000 Avios per person. **70,000 Avios** gets both of you to London.
* **The Return (KrisFlyer/Star Alliance):** Use your **1.1 Lakh KrisFlyer Miles**. KrisFlyer allows you to book Star Alliance partners (like Air India, Swiss, or Lufthansa). A one-way Economy ticket from Europe to India is ~45,000 miles. **90,000 KrisFlyer Miles** brings both of you back home.

## 🗺️ Trip 2: Switzerland (The Accor Shield)
*Hotels are charged per room, so your points don't lose value when traveling as a couple!*

* **Flights:** If this is a separate trip from London, transfer your **56,000 Axis ERP** to **Air India** (giving you 84,800 AI points total). This is almost exactly enough for two one-way Economy tickets (90k needed) on Air India's direct flight to Zurich.
* **Hotels (Crucial):** Use your **72,000 Accor points**. Your 72k points equal a flat **€1,440 (~₹1.3 Lakhs)**. This will completely wipe out the punishing cash rates at a Swissôtel or Fairmont for 3 to 4 nights for you and your wife.

## 🗺️ Trip 3: Vietnam (The "Pay Cash" Optimization)
*Do NOT use points for Vietnam flights.*

* **Flights:** Cash flights from India to Vietnam (via VietJet or Indigo) are incredibly cheap (often ₹15,000 - ₹20,000 round-trip per person). Wasting your premium Singapore Airlines miles on a budget route is a terrible return on investment. **Pay cash for these flights.**
* **Exception:** If you want a luxury experience, you can burn 104,000 KrisFlyer miles for **Two One-Way Business Class** tickets to Vietnam on Singapore Airlines, and pay cash for the budget flight back.

## 🗺️ Trip 4: China (The Marriott Luxury Arbitrage)
*Marriott points are insanely overpowered in China, and 1 room covers both of you.*

* **Flights:** Pay cash. Flights via Cathay Pacific or VietJet are relatively cheap.
* **Hotels:** Use your **1.5 Lakh Marriott Bonvoy points**. Marriott offers the "5th Night Free" on award bookings. 150k points will easily secure a 5-night stay at a top-tier luxury property (like The St. Regis, W Hotel, or JW Marriott in Beijing/Shanghai) for you and your wife.

---
### 💡 Strategy Summary for 2 Pax:
* Because points requirements double for two people, Business class redemptions are mostly off the table for Europe unless you buy a lot of points. 
* Your massive hotel balances (Marriott and Accor) are your greatest assets because **hotel costs don't double for a couple**. They will save you upwards of **₹2.5 Lakhs** in cash across Switzerland and China.
