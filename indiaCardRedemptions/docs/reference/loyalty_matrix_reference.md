## 7. Closed-Loop & Co-Branded Cards
The following cards in your portfolio do **NOT** have transfer partners. They are "closed-loop," meaning their rewards are hard-coded to automatically dump into their specific partner's ecosystem or app:

*   **IndusInd Qatar Airways (Avios Visa Infinite):** Automatically sweeps your points as Avios directly into Qatar Airways Privilege Club or British Airways Executive Club.
*   **HDFC Marriott Bonvoy:** Automatically sweeps points directly into your Marriott Bonvoy account.
*   **HDFC Tata Neu (Infinity/Plus):** Earns NeuCoins (1 NeuCoin = ₹1). Usable only within the Tata Neu ecosystem (BigBasket, 1mg, AirAsia India, Taj Hotels, Croma).
*   **IndusInd EazyDiner:** Earns EazyDiner Prime points, strictly usable for restaurant discounts on the EazyDiner app.
*   **SBI BPCL:** Points are strictly redeemable for fuel at Bharat Petroleum (BPCL) pumps.
*   **Indigo 6E Rewards (HDFC/Kotak):** Points are directly credited as 6E Rewards for booking IndiGo flights.
