# The Points Array - Master Feature List & PRD

**Official Product Name:** The Points Array
**Core Mission:** To maximize travel rewards and points yield for Indian credit card holders by tracking milestones, calculating transfer arbitrage ratios, and providing AI-powered route planning to sweet-spot airline and hotel redemptions.

---

## 🎨 Design System & Aesthetics (Theme Rules)
*   **Theme**: Clean light theme / white background.
*   **Colors**: Corporate navy blue and metallic silver accents for tabs, buttons, borders, highlights, and icons.
*   **Typography**: Modern high-contrast premium typography, clean layouts, and soft shadows.

---

## 📱 Feature 1: The Digital Wallet (Milestone Tracker)
*   **Card Catalog**: Support for premium Indian credit cards (HDFC Infinia, Axis Atlas/Magnus, American Express Platinum, SBI Aurum, ICICI Emeralde).
*   **Points Ledger**: Real-time logging of Reward Points, Edge Miles, and Membership Rewards.
*   **Milestone Timeline**: Consolidated trackers showing annual/quarterly spending progress bars (e.g., Axis Atlas ₹3L/₹7.5L tiers, Amex ₹4L Platinum Travel milestone) side-by-side.

---

## 🧮 Feature 2: Indian Transfer Matrix Engine
*   **Dynamic Ratio Mapping**: Programmatic logic reflecting actual Indian transfer ratios (e.g., Axis Edge Miles 5:4 to Marriott Bonvoy/Accor; HDFC Infinia 1:1 to British Airways/Accor).
*   **Transfer Duration Alerts**: Displays estimated transfer times (e.g., "Amex to Marriott: Instant", "Axis to Flying Blue: 48-72 hours").
*   **Live Multiplier Promos**: Highlights active transfer bonuses (e.g., Marriott's +30% transfer bonus campaigns) to identify accelerated redemptions.

---

## 🏨 Feature 3: Hotel Arbitrage Yield Calculator
*   **Arbitrage Logic**: Evaluates manual user inputs for Hotel Name, Cash Price (in INR), and Points Cost (from loyalty programs).
*   **Valuation Output**: Computes the exact cash value per point (INR yield).
*   **Visual Overlay**: Displays a clear badge ("RECOMMENDED" in corporate navy blue) for high-yield bookings (> ₹1.5 per point) or warning labels for poor redemption values.
*   **Point Sale Arbitrage Alerts**: Tracks active discount and bonus promotions on direct purchases of airline/hotel points (e.g. Hyatt 40% bonus, Marriott 35% bonus). Automatically computes if buying points is cheaper than booking with cash and triggers savings alerts.
*   **Deep-Linked Partner Bookings**: Dynamic buttons on recommended deals that deep-link users directly to the airline or hotel search engines (e.g. Marriott Bonvoy or Virgin Atlantic) with pre-filled destination, dates, and ticket classes.

---

## 📊 Feature 4: Insights Hub & MCC Checker
*   **ROI Simulator**: Slide-based annual spend and annual fee inputs to graph net point yield cash values against annual card costs.
*   **Merchant Category Code (MCC) Checker**: Instant search engine mapping Indian merchants (e.g., Swiggy, Zomato, utility providers) to card-specific reward multipliers (e.g., 5X/10X accelerators) to avoid rewards caps.
*   **Net Savings Calculator**: Shows exact net savings metrics (e.g., "Net Annual Savings: ₹35,000").
*   **Programmatic HNW Portfolio Audit**: An automated portfolio scanner analyzing the user's spending profile, annual card fees, and reward yields. If the yield is sub-optimal (< 5% return), the app highlights the leaked value and funnels the user to book a paid consultation.

---

## 🤖 Feature 5: AI Concierge (AwardHack AI)
*   **Contextual Strategy Chat**: Conversational interface passing the user's active wallet balance, target destinations, and matrix rates directly to the LLM (Gemini).
*   **Graphical Routing Timeline**: Visual step-by-step maps detailing points transfer routes (e.g., "Transfer 60k Amex to Marriott for flight partners") to reach the target seat/room.
*   **Carrier Fee Warning Heuristic**: Informs users of hidden tax/fuel surcharges (e.g., "Warning: British Airways passes on high surcharges; transfer to Aeroplan instead").

---

## 📅 Feature 6: Strategy Consultation Booking
*   **Schedule Portal**: Seamless scheduler tool for booking customized 1-on-1 strategy calls with award travel experts.
*   **Assisted Concierge Booking**: Expert handholding service to handle complex points transfers and secure award reservations on the customer's behalf.

---

## 🥞 Feature 7: Multi-Layer Stacking Visualizer
*   **Multi-Layer Integration**: Visual flowchart guiding users through stacking Layer 1 (credit card points), Layer 2 (bank reward portals like HDFC SmartBuy / Axis Grab Deals), and Layer 3 (merchant loyalty programs like Marriott Bonvoy).
*   **Yield Maximization**: Highlights the exact payment routing sequence to achieve double/triple-dip returns on everyday expenses.
