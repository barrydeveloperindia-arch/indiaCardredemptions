## 🔍 Program Deep-Dives: What Actually Makes Sense?

### 1. Air Canada Aeroplan (The Ultimate M4B Partner)
*   **Why it's amazing:** They do **NOT** pass on fuel surcharges. If you book a Lufthansa or Swiss Business Class flight through Aeroplan, you pay just the miles + a flat CAD $39 partner booking fee and standard airport taxes. 
*   **For London/Switzerland:** A couple in Business Class will need **120,000 to 140,000 Aeroplan points** one-way. That is just **2.5 months** of your M4B point generation!
*   **For Vietnam:** Booking Singapore Airlines Business Class via Aeroplan for **30k–45k points** is an absolute steal.

### 2. Air France-KLM Flying Blue (The Promo Champion)
*   **Why it's amazing:** Every month, they release "Promo Rewards" offering up to 50% off select routes. If a Mumbai/Delhi route is featured, Business Class can drop to **~40k miles per person**.
*   **The Catch:** Outside of promos, the dynamic pricing can be brutal, and their carrier-imposed fuel surcharges mean you will pay **₹30k to ₹40k cash in taxes per ticket** for Business Class.
*   **When to transfer:** *Only* when you spot a Flying Blue Promo Reward for your exact dates. Otherwise, stick to Aeroplan.

### 3. Turkish Miles&Smiles (The High-Risk/High-Reward Play)
*   **Why it's amazing:** At **45,000 miles** for a Star Alliance Business Class one-way to Europe, it is the cheapest chart in existence.
*   **The Catch:** Finding 2 Business Class seats on Turkish or Lufthansa is incredibly rare, the taxes are high, and the online engine is notorious for failing during checkout.
