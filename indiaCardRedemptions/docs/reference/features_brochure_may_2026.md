# The Points Array - Product Features Brochure
**Publication Date:** May 22, 2026
**Target Audience:** Premium Travel & Credit Card Rewards Enthusiasts in India

---

## 🌟 Executive Summary

**The Points Array** is India's premium, next-generation credit card reward milestone tracking and transfer arbitrage platform. Designed specifically for high-spend credit card holders (HDFC Infinia, Axis Atlas/Magnus, American Express Platinum, SBI Aurum), the platform takes the complexity out of points collection and award travel bookings. 

By calculating conversion yields, tracking milestones across different issuers on a single timeline, and offering local statement parsing with AI strategy routing, we turn credit card spending into high-yield luxury travel.

---

## 📱 The Seven Core Pillars of the Platform

### 1. The Dynamic Digital Wallet & Milestone Timeline
Track all your credit cards, reward points, and active spend milestones in one beautiful dashboard.
*   **Multi-Issuer Aggregation**: Supports top Indian premium cards (Amex, HDFC, Axis, SBI, ICICI) with unified point totals.
*   **Unified Milestone Tracks**: Visual progress dials showing spend tiers (e.g., Axis Atlas ₹3L/₹7.5L/₹15L, Amex Plat Travel ₹1.9L/₹4L) side-by-side.
*   **Actionable Alerts**: Never miss a milestone. The app flags upcoming cycle deadlines (rolling card anniversaries vs. calendar quarters).

### 2. Live Indian Transfer Matrix Engine
Direct conversion logic translating Indian credit card points into global airline and hotel miles.
*   **Dynamic Ratio Mapping**: Natively understands specific Indian conversion rules (e.g., Axis Edge Miles 5:4 to Marriott, HDFC Infinia 1:1 to Accor/Singapore Krisflyer).
*   **Devaluation Protection**: Automatically updates ratio coefficients in real time when banks modify programs, protecting your portfolio's value.
*   **Transfer Duration Logs**: Displays real-world transfer times (e.g., "Amex to Marriott: Instant", "Axis to Flying Blue: 48 Hours") to help you time seat bookings.

### 3. Hotel & Flight Arbitrage Yield Calculator
Stop guessing whether points redemptions are worth it compared to cash rates.
*   **Yield Calculator**: Input Cash Price vs. Points Cost, and the engine computes the exact "Value per Point" (VPP) in INR.
*   **Point Purchase Arbitrage**: Compares the cash price of a stay against direct point-purchase sales (e.g., Hyatt 40% bonus sales) to guide you on when to buy points directly for massive discounts.
*   **Navy Blue Highlight Badges**: Instantly marks high-value opportunities with a "RECOMMENDED" badge.
*   **Deep-Linked Partner Bookings**: Preloaded search query redirection buttons on recommended deals that deep-link users directly to official booking engines (e.g. Marriott Bonvoy or Virgin Atlantic) with dates, class, and destination prefilled.

### 4. Insights Hub & Multiplier MCC Search Tool
Maximize the rewards you earn on every single swipe.
*   **Merchant Category Code (MCC) Search**: Instant search for Indian merchants (Swiggy, Zomato, Amazon, utility bills) showing which card in your wallet gives the highest multiplier rate (e.g., 5X/10X accelerators) and flags active reward caps.
*   **ROI Spend Simulator**: Slide-based calculations graphing card annual fees against points values to show net annual savings.

### 5. Multi-Layer Stacking Visualizer
Unleash the power of double and triple dipping.
*   **Visual Flowcharts**: Illustrates exact purchasing routes (e.g., Buy Gyftr voucher on HDFC SmartBuy -> Earn 5X base points -> Redeem via Marriott during +30% promo).
*   **Compounded Yield Calculation**: Shows the combined total return on spend across all reward channels in a single screen.

### 6. Secure Local Statement Parser
The ultimate utility for instant milestone updates with total privacy.
*   **100% On-Device Parsing**: Import PDF statements. Decryption and text processing are executed locally inside the sandbox. No personal or financial details are uploaded or stored.
*   **Encrypted File Support**: Prompts for statement passwords, processing decryption entirely in volatile memory.

### 7. AI Travel Concierge (AwardHack AI)
Your conversational routing copilot powered by Gemini.
*   **Contextual Routing Engine**: The AI reads your current points balances and transfer routes to generate step-by-step itineraries:
    > *"To book BOM to Paris in Business Class, transfer 30k Amex points to Marriott, then convert 60k Marriott points to Flying Blue for the lowest surcharges."*
*   **Carrier Fee Warnings**: Flags high tax and fuel surcharges to avoid surprise out-of-pocket costs.
